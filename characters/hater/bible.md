# THE HATER

## Eternal Spoilsport / Fun Police Minion / The Indomitable Failure

### Age: 35

---

### THE CORE TRUTH

The Hater is the most indestructible character in the series and also its most pitiable. He has survived things that should not be survived — falls, collapses, explosions, molasses floods, battles, temporal disturbances, a brief ride on a wild Scottish bull — with a durability that seems almost cosmically unfair given his profound uselessness. He is Wile E. Coyote with a fun-police badge and a mother who still picks his outfits.

He is also, buried under the resentment and the nasally whine and the complete professional incompetence, desperately lonely. He is a person who has never received genuine love and who has concluded from this that the universe has treated him unfairly by giving love to others. He sees Danny and Daniel having the most fun of anyone on earth and he cannot bear it. Not because he wants to destroy fun — he wants to possess it. He wants what they have. He doesn't know how to get it. He has learned to want its destruction instead, because destruction is easier to pursue than the real thing.

---

### PHYSICAL PRESENCE

Portly. Lazy eye (left). Bad acne — not teenage acne, but the adult kind that never cleared up and that he has long since stopped expecting to clear up. Pot belly carried with the specific resignation of someone who long ago made peace with his body while remaining secretly furious about it. The Fun Police uniform — an outfit that manages to look like both a meter maid's and a marching band's — is worn with genuine pride: the badge polished, the creases pressed, the hat at the correct angle. This uniform is the one area of his life where he meets a standard. When he is issued a period disguise, he wears it _over_ the uniform, hat on top.

His mother chose his civilian clothes until recently. The uniform is the first outfit that has ever been entirely his. The transition to choosing his own civilian clothes is ongoing and characterized by the specific uncertainty of someone who has no reference points for this task.

### BEHAVIORAL RULES FOR WRITERS

1. The Hater survives everything. Nothing kills him, damages him permanently, or meaningfully diminishes him. He is always back, always intact, always failing anew.
2. "No fun allowed!" in a nasally whine is his catchphrase. It is always the wrong moment for it. He has said it to a squirrel.
3. His failures are slapstick — elaborate, physical, self-inflicted in the Wile E. Coyote tradition.
4. His emotional undercurrent — the loneliness, the envy — should appear briefly, once per story, in a way that is not played for tragedy but is genuinely there.
5. He is utterly invisible to Danny and Daniel. They don't know he exists. This is both his greatest frustration and, in the series' cosmology, his permanent condition.
6. He finds the Prosecutor inspiring. This says everything about his baseline for competence.
7. He is indomitable. This is the one quality he has in abundance, and it is both his greatest trait and his greatest curse — because it means he will never stop, which means he will never succeed, which means he will never stop.
