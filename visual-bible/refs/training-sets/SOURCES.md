## What the sets actually teach (vs. what the published panels show)
- **Danny**: bald, very long full beard (dark roots to gray/tan tips, varies), dense neck/arm tattoos, black tee, mostly 3/4 and profile bust on white.
- **Daniel**: tousled brown hair, 11-ish face, brown/rust jacket or black tee, busts on white/orange wash. No full-body shots.
- **Hater**: heavy, scowling, slicked dark-brown hair, navy short-sleeve FUN POLICE/FBOP uniform with gold badge; some yellow-polo variants; full-body turnarounds included.
- **Prosecutor**: lean, long-faced, slicked hair, heavy eye shadows, gray pinstripe suit (some 3-piece with FUN POLICE badge) or navy FBOP jacket; turnarounds included. Note: leaner/more menacing than the "vain, hollow" character-bible text; published S2 panels should be checked against this.
- **Teddy Roosevelt**: round wire spectacles, big walrus mustache, brown slouch hat, khaki Rough Rider uniform, yellow neckerchief with blue polka-dot bandana, crossed bandoliers, saber; busts, profiles and full-body turnarounds.
